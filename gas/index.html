    How to read this

    • Site value is total gas volume times the snapshot ISK per m³. It is the whole cloud, not what you can carry out.
    • Haul for your stay takes the priciest gas first at your ship's rate and stops at your hold size or the gas in the site. Venture and Prospect run about 9,600 m³ per hour with T2 scoops, which matches the wiki's clear times. The Pioneer, Pioneer Consortium Issue and Outrider rates are my estimates: the wiki lists no gas yield bonus for them, and no gas figures at all for the Outrider. Confirm in the in-game ship info.
    • Sleepers arrive about 15 to 20 minutes after you land. Past 15 minutes on the slider, plan to be gone.
    • Each reaction takes 100 units of each fullerite, 5 fuel blocks and one mineral, and returns about 40% of the input volume as hybrid polymer. Polymers then go into hybrid tech components for Tech 3 hulls and subsystems. The wiki does not say which polymer feeds which component.
    • The wiki's text lists C540 as the second input for C3-FTM Acid, but its icon is labelled C320-540. Check in game before you buy.
    • Sleeper stats are as listed on each site page, usually Wave 1 only. The wiki's wormhole sites page is flagged as needing a refresh for the 2021 mining field changes.

    Sources: Gas cloud harvesting, Wormhole sites, Reactions, Tech 3 Production, Fuzzwork gas prices, and each reservoir's own wiki page.
