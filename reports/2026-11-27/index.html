Headlines

  • Most kills (final blows): a tie between Docteur West (3) and Movmax (3). dmacc was on 11 of the 16 kills.
  • Most damage: dmacc with 47,112 (about 30% of the named gang's damage), then Movmax with 38,187. Movmax's 15,536 on the Caracal Navy Issue was the biggest single-kill output.
  • Biggest kills: Myrmidon Navy Issue (221.7M), Caracal Navy Issue (136.3M), Augoror Navy Issue (133.4M), Coercer Navy Issue (67.9M), Thrasher Fleet Issue (60.2M).
  • Losses (3): Docteur West's Caldari Navy Hookbill (28.1M, 06:40) and mufaxor's Algos Navy Issue (43.4M, 06:45), both in Vard, plus Rosidah Rahim's Federation Navy Comet (19.2M, 08:33, Huola), killed by Juan Mileghere, whose Vexor died a minute later (kill 18).
