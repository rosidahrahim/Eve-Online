How to read this

  • Months for cash is the cheapest exact mix of 1, 3, 6, 12 and 24 month Omega packages at the cash prices above. With the Markee box ticked each price drops 3%.
  • Buying PLEX with cash first finds the cheapest mix of PLEX packs that holds enough PLEX for the PLEX route, so it may leave a few spare PLEX.
  • ISK figures are PLEX count times the PLEX price you set. They are the sell price of PLEX, not a guarantee you can fill the order.
  • The Omega and bundle prices are the sale prices from 19 Nov 2026. The sale ended on 26 Nov, so recheck before you buy.
  • Three starter packs (Frontline Cadet, Empire Guardian, Nexus Reckoning) show a lower skill points per dollar figure in the sheet because it counts only the booster skill points. This page counts the bought skill points too.
  • The sheet labels the 1,500 and 6,000 PLEX skill bundles Undock and Warp. Their skill point totals match the Afterburn and Jump bundles, so they are labelled that way here.
  • The sheet's Skill Extractor "bounceback" columns are left out. Their figures use the 12 Sep extractor price, so they do not reflect today's price.
