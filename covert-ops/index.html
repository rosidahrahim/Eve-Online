Covert Ops Hacking Comparison

Helios, Anathema, Cheetah and Imicus Navy Issue side by side, laid out like the in-game compare window, with a verdict on which one hacks relic and data sites best.

Base values from the in-game compare window: no skills, modules or rigs. Prices are estimated ISK as shown in the client on the day of capture (2026-11-25).

Verdict: Helios

Helios, Anathema and Cheetah all carry the same +10 Analyzer Virus Strength Bonus and the 5s cloak delay. Helios wins the tie-break with the most CPU (290 tf), the most raw HP of the covert ops (1,618) and five mid slots. Imicus Navy Issue is out for hacking: its bonus is only +5 and it isn't flagged for the Covert Ops cloak.

Which to pick

  • Helios is the all-round hacker: top CPU, the toughest covert ops hull and a spare mid slot beyond the prop mod. It has only 3 lows, so it can't carry as many low-slot Warp Core Stabilizers or align mods as the 4-low hulls.
  • Cheetah if escape speed matters more than staying power: best top speed, inertia and scan resolution, but the weakest tank and lowest powergrid (15 MW).
  • Anathema is a fine alternative with four lows for Warp Core Stabilizers or align mods, but it is the slowest and has the least structure.
  • Imicus Navy Issue is the cheap hauler: 400 m3 cargo, drones and a third rig slot. It is the weakest hacker here (+5 bonus, no covert cloak) so keep it for quiet space.

Other suggestions

  • Add the Buzzard to the comparison. EVE University lists it as the strongest pure scanner/hacker thanks to its 5 mid / 3 low layout and scan deviation bonus. It's the one covert ops hull missing from this sheet.
  • Train Covert Ops. Each level cuts the CPU cost of the Covert Ops cloak by 20%, which matters most on the tight-CPU hulls.
  • Check the hacking rigs. Rigs such as the Memetic Algorithm Bank (data) and Emission Scope Sharpener (relic) raise virus strength. Compare their numbers in the fitting window before buying.
  • Compare with skills applied. Re-run this comparison with your own skills loaded, since align time, cloak CPU and hacking strength all shift with training.
